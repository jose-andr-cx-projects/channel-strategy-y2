# Experience Concepts and Validation

Status: Working concept; first prototype scope and validation pending
Last updated: 27 November 2026

## Executive summary

The Intelligent Front Door aims to help customers understand their situation and move confidently to the right existing service pathway, regardless of where they begin. Parking infringements are the documented first Design case, not an approved whole-of-council implementation.

**Recognise → Understand → Personalise → Orchestrate**

| Concept stage | Intended experience |
|---|---|
| Recognise | Identify the customer's intent and relevant situation in their own language. |
| Understand | Explain applicable information, options, evidence and verified expectations. |
| Personalise | Ask only contextual questions that change guidance or support. |
| Orchestrate | Present the appropriate existing pathway and clear handoff, with accessible assistance. |

Pay, nominate and review are candidate pathways. Comply remains the primary quality lens: fair, transparent and easy to resolve. Transact and Apply are used where relevant; do not infer a mode solely from a pathway label.

## Validation priorities

Select one parking scenario with stakeholders and test correct pathway selection, confidence in the next step and unnecessary navigation steps. Confirm rules, representation and authority with the service owner. Separately explore Track my case through webmessenger and evidence-backed webpage improvements. Do not assume automation, eligibility decisions, system integration or measured benefits.
