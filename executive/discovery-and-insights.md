# Discovery and Insights

Status: Evidence synthesis; current baseline reconciliation required
Last updated: 27 November 2026

## Executive summary

Discovery reframed the challenge from optimising channels independently to connecting customer intent, information, service pathways and appropriate support. Earlier analysis identified difficulty finding information and understanding processes as important sources of friction. The priority is to reduce avoidable effort without removing useful human assistance.

## Evidence and implications

| Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|
| Q1 analysis reported approximately 77% of identified website issues related to finding information or understanding a process | Improve discoverability and guidance before assuming customers need another channel. |
| Q1 analysis reported approximately 55% of General Enquiries calls were information-seeking | Investigate where assisted contact compensates for unclear information or pathways. |
| August 2024 parking review research identified uncertainty about grounds, evidence, next steps and progress | Test clearer intent-led guidance and expectations; revalidate current rules and conditions. |
| September off-platform search assessment method established | Assess discoverability, authority, accuracy, currency, understandability and actionability separately. Actual results require reconciliation. |

The Q1 percentages are historical discovery signals from the earlier project-management export, not current performance baselines. The parking research concerns review customers and cannot be generalised to all infringement customers.

## Opportunities

Connect upstream discovery to the right authoritative service; use task-level interaction and support lenses; strengthen reusable service naming and knowledge ownership; protect assisted and accessible pathways.
