# Project outcomes and scope

## Intended outcome
Improve channel experience and interaction pathways through bounded discovery, design and validation

## Scope lenses
Interaction modes; segments; intelligent front door; webforms; digital-channel alignment; accessibility

## Related work
CX Service Experience Mapping; Business Context Architecture

## Working boundaries
- The project retains its own subject matter and evidence. Shared portfolio conventions concern **navigation**, **provenance**, **approval** and **execution**, not a mandatory delivery method.
- No previous implementation result, milestone or approval is inferred to have survived the new Bitbucket-ready staging reset.
- Populate the current operational baseline from approved Bitbucket, Confluence and Jira records after import.
